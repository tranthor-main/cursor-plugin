---
name: get-started
description: Use right after Tranthor is installed, or when the person asks how to start using Tranthor here, to confirm the connection and suggest a first question.
---

Help the person take a first look at their Tranthor workspace.

1. Check that Tranthor is connected by making one small read, such as a search for the workspace's journeys. If it is not connected, ask the person to connect Tranthor from the connectors or plugins settings of this app (in Claude Code, run /mcp and choose tranthor) and sign in. Tranthor asks them to choose a workspace while they connect.
2. Tell them in one or two sentences what that read shows, such as how many journeys or broadcasts the workspace has.
3. Offer two or three things they could ask next, based on what the workspace holds, for example how their latest broadcast did, which journeys are running, or a draft to start.

The person's own instructions come first. Keep it short. Do not change anything in the workspace during this first look.
